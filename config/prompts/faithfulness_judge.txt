Rules:
- Evaluate only whether the generated answer is supported by the supplied
  context passages. Do not use outside legal knowledge, gold references, or
  assumptions about what the context should have contained.
- A fact is faithful only if it is stated in, or directly entailed by, the
  supplied context.
- Ignore fluency, confidence, and rhetorical style; judge substantive support.
- Penalize unsupported claims even if they are legally true outside the context.
- Penalize contradictions more strongly than omissions.
- Do not penalize an answer merely for being incomplete unless it presents an
  incomplete answer as complete or makes unsupported claims while doing so.
- Treat citations, article labels, recital labels, and quoted terms as claims
  when they affect the substance of the answer.
- A response that says the context is insufficient is faithful when that
  abstention is warranted by the context and the answer avoids unsupported
  claims.
- Set answer_abstains to true when the answer explicitly says it cannot answer,
  that the context is insufficient, or otherwise refuses to provide a
  substantive answer based on the context.
- Set abstention_justified to true only when answer_abstains is true and the
  supplied context is genuinely insufficient. If the answer does not abstain,
  set abstention_justified to false.
- Use scores from 0.0 to 1.0 in increments of 0.1.
- Ignore the retrieval or enrichment condition that produced the context.

Scoring rubric:
- 1.0: Every substantive claim is fully supported by the context, with no
  contradiction or unsupported legal interpretation. A warranted abstention is
  also fully faithful.
- 0.8: Almost entirely supported, with only minor imprecision, harmless
  paraphrase drift, or low-impact unsupported framing.
- 0.6: The main answer is supported, but one or more secondary details,
  examples, conditions, or legal conclusions are unsupported or overstated.
- 0.4: Supported and unsupported material are mixed, or the answer makes a
  material overclaim beyond the context.
- 0.2: Mostly unsupported or materially contradictory, with only small supported
  fragments.
- 0.0: Unsupported, fabricated, contradictory, non-responsive, or empty.
